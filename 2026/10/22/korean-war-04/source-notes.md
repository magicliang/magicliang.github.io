主要来源：[Appleman, South to the Naktong, North to the Yalu](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D114-PURL-gpo93269/pdf/GOVPUB-D114-PURL-gpo93269.pdf)。已下载PDF，用pypdf提取并阅读下列章节相关段落，不宣称通读全书。
