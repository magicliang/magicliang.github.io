The three Survey of Palestine maps are identified as Crown works by their
printed official authorship; their original copyright term has expired.
Do not treat the recent scan/upload date as the original work's creation date.
Primary Crown copyright information and duration guidance:
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/information-management/re-using-public-sector-information/uk-government-licensing-framework/crown-copyright/
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/information-management/crown-copyright-flowchart.pdf

un-partition-plan-1947-map103.gif
United Nations, Map No.103.1(b), February 1956.
Map depicts the 1947 partition proposal, not actual 1956 frontiers.
Base map: Survey of Palestine, April 1946, modified by the original producer.
Original resource: http://domino.un.org/maps/m0103_1b.gif
Downloaded original image from:
https://web.archive.org/web/20021218133456id_/http://domino.un.org/maps/m0103_1b.gif
No local modifications. Not declared public domain.
Credit in the web caption: United Nations Geospatial Information Section.
Link the credit to https://www.un.org/geospatial .
The UN map permission page expressly addresses hosting map copies on websites
with attribution and a link. It separately describes permission applications
for book/article republication; this note does not confer print permissions.
https://www.un.org/geospatial/es/mandates/public
